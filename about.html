About Me

Hi, I'm Stephanie!

I am a senior at Kennesaw State University pursuing a degree in Software Engineering. I have a strong interest in UI/UX design and enjoy creating intuitive, user-friendly experiences that combine functionality with thoughtful design. Throughout my academic career, I have participated in multiple hackathons, giving me the opportunity to strengthen my problem-solving skills, collaborate with others, and develop projects in fast-paced environments. I am passionate about continuing to expand my technical and design skills and look forward to applying them to projects that create meaningful and engaging user experiences.
